About Us

Welcome to Mindful Meditation, your online space for relaxation, mindfulness and wellbeing.

We believe everyone deserves time to focus on themselves. Our online yoga sessions make it easy to practise from the comfort of your own home, whether you are a beginner or an experienced yogi.

Choose between friendly group classes or personalised one-to-one sessions with our instructors. Our aim is to help you build a healthier mind and body, one session at a time.
